# Mission Flag — Project Chat Handoff (PR-01)
**Use this as the pinned kickoff instruction in the Mission Flag project chat.**

We are prioritizing **Mission Flag** as a dedicated, Double Good-inspired, closed virtual fundraising and wholesale flag platform. This is not a public generic flag marketplace and not separate Shopify stores for dealers. The central Shopify backend powers guest checkout and order management; our Mission Flag app owns campaigns, proofs, approved partner access, sharing, earnings and operations.

**Canonical GitHub planning documents:**
- Product source of truth: `docs/MISSION_FLAG_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`
- Batch execution plan: `docs/PR-01_EXECUTION_PLAN.md`
- Repo: `nine89graphics/MissionFlag`

**Must preserve:** approved dealer + organizer roles; campaigns lasting 7 or 14 days; 12 exact signature style choices after mascot/logo upload and school colors; flagship garden, house and 3×5 flags in first paid MVP and six sizes modeled; direct-to-home shipping; participant share links and guest checkout; real Shopify order/webhook tracking; accurate group/dealer earnings and Mission Flag retained contribution; separate wholesale flag ordering with correct C-coded trade net pricing.

**Twelve styles:** Varsity Classic; Velocity; Predator; Vortex; Gridiron / Sport Pattern; Splatter; Ice Breaker; Tie-Dye Spirit; Paint Drip; Shield Pride; Digital Camo; Neon Energy. Do not confuse twelve selectable template styles with the old implementation capacity of up to 12 active program designs.

**Intake:** collect organization, school mascot/logo and colors, fundraising purpose and dates, plus progressive optional reach metrics including number of schools, students, teams, athletes/players, households, alumni and available promotion channels. Calculate transparent low/base/high projections without promising results or collecting minors' PII.

**Speed rules:** Recover/reuse existing Mission Flag/Fundraiser Flags Shopify app source first. Preserve tested functionality. Implement cohesive batches and automated regression. Demonstrate a complete end-to-end fundraiser + wholesale order only at clear approval gates. Do not repeatedly stop after each file/button/test. Do not change unrelated 989Engraving/B2BBranded projects. Require owner approval only for irreversible, legal, payment, credential, production deployment and go-live decisions.

**Next task after PR-01 approval:** Audit/recover source, then deliver foundation/Batches 0–1 in PR-02 with a consolidated status and tests. Verify staging checkout, proof process, 12 styles, accurate economics and fulfillment before real transactions.

**Important:** GitHub plans describe intended features; they are not evidence of a deployed working application.
